# Dummy Chrome Browser

A Dummy Chrome Browser for TCL Flip 2.

## Introducing Chrome… ish.

Meet the world's most secure browser. Click any link, and in a split second it takes you to… absolutely nowhere. Instead, you get one of eight hand-animated scenes: a barrier arm slamming down on a car, a padlock rattling, a spinning globe with a big red stop sign, a robot bonking into a wall, a plug that just won't fit, and more. Every one tells you politely that your device is restricted from browsing the internet.

- 100% malware-proof. No page loads, so there's nothing to catch.
- Zero data usage, zero tracking, zero cookies.
- Blazing fast. Nothing has ever loaded faster than nothing.
- Eight different animations, so even being denied never gets boring.
- A tiny download, about 25 KB.

No ads. No distractions. No browsing. Just peace of mind, and a very expressive robot.

**Chrome… ish. The internet isn't for everyone.**
